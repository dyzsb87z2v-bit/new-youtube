# So What Happened? — Brand kit (English channel)

Channel language: **English**, for a global audience. Planning notes in this repo stay in Persian; everything published (name, titles, voiceover, captions, descriptions) is English.

## Name & handles
- **Channel name:** So What Happened?
- **Handle (in order of preference):** `@sowhathappened` · `@so.what.happened` · `@sowhathappenedshorts`
- Use the same handle on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. Availability not yet checked.

## Format (fixed)
- Every episode is **exactly 60 seconds**, vertical 9:16.
- Opens with the date card **THEN: [year] → NOW: [year]** and the question.
- Ends with a complete answer. No cliffhangers, no "part 2".
- Two 10-second reconstruction clips per episode, always labelled **RECONSTRUCTION**.
- Everything else is licensed images, motion graphics, narration and editing.
- No paid tools.

## Topic rule
Subjects go first to stories with **public, freely licensed archives**: space missions, public projects, Olympics and stadiums, infrastructure, public places, products with Commons coverage.
Stories about private individuals are the exception, never the default (see channel bible §7).

## Title formula
`[Known fact]. [The question]?` — under ~60 characters.
- The Olympics Ended. What Happened to the Stadium?
- It Was Supposed to Replace the Car. So What Happened?
- This Mall Closed. What's Inside Today?
- Launched in 1977. Where Is It Now?
