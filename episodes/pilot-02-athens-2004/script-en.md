The Olympics ended. What happened to the venues?

THEN: 2004 — NOW: 2026
Athens built a sports complex on its old airport for the 2004 Games. Ten years later, much of it stood abandoned. In 2014 the site was handed to developers; today it is becoming a new district, the Ellinikon.
